How to make this app run on macOS when it complains:

"Can't be opened because the identity of the developer cannot be confirmed"

* Put FooBar.app in your home folder (the parent of Documents / Desktop)
* Open up the Terminal app
* Type: xattr -r -d com.apple.quarantine FooBar.app
* Press enter
* Close Terminal

Apologies that this is necessary, but Apple wants to charge $150/year to
developers to 'sign' their apps to make these warnings go away.

* Optional alternative that might be relevant in future macos:
    codesign --force --deep --sign - FooBar.app
